What it does

OFC Calculator keeps score for Open Face Chinese Poker (Pineapple) tables of 2–4 players. Enter the rows each player won, royalties and fouls for every hand, and the app works out the points, scoop bonuses and running totals. It only counts points: there is no money, betting or real-money play of any kind.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere.

How is a scoop scored?
Winning all three rows against an opponent pays the scoop value you set for the table (6 points by default, adjustable when you create it).

What happens on a foul?
A fouled hand is scooped by every opponent who did not foul. With “Foul pays opponent’s royalties” on, the fouling player also pays the opponents’ royalties.

How do I move my games to a new phone?
Games are kept on the device; use the Share button on the results screen to save or send a summary.
