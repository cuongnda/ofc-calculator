SupportPrivacy Policy

Summary

OFC Calculator does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Data on your device

Table names, player names and scores you enter are saved only in the app’s storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

No tracking

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs and makes no network requests.

Sharing

If you tap Share, the iOS share sheet sends the text summary only to the destination you choose.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
